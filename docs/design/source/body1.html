What this is, and what it is not

Settled

The ten colours, the three typefaces, glassmorphism per iOS 26, three themes, macOS-native motion, the adaptive-colour rule, and the structural behaviour of the dock, the island and the face. You specified these outright.

Derived

The eleven ambience modes. You sent eleven images and said to extract the feel; each one became exactly one mode, with its palette pulled from the actual pixels rather than described from memory.

Waiting

The UI references. The images here are feel, mark and face — not interface references. Nothing below commits the actual look of a screen; the primitives are set, the composition is not.

One thing taken out of the face reference. It carries invented telemetry — REASONING 110%, MEMORY ARCHIVE 241-F8-J-1000 PB, panels of lorem. VESPER's own INV-HW-2 says a number is measured or it is UNKNOWN, and UNKNOWN means unavailable. So the form is taken and the fiction is refused: the panels around the face show real thinking, real tasks and real numbers, or they show nothing at all.

The eleven sources

Feel, mark, face.

Ten of the eleven are night. The one interior and the one daylight frame are what the light theme is built from.

01 · Palette

Ten colours.

Your ten, with the OKLCH coordinates they will actually be manipulated in — because the adaptive rule moves lightness while holding hue, and that is only safe in a perceptual space.

Contrast figures are WCAG ratios against the two grounds. They decide which colours may carry text and which may only carry light.

Two colours are grounds, not accents. #03141f and #ffeae3 sit at 16.1:1 against each other and never appear as an accent on their own ground. Cream and orange cannot carry body text on light — 1.12:1 and 1.53:1. They are light sources, not ink. Ember is the only colour that reads on both grounds (4.49 / 3.59), which is why it is the light theme's accent.
