Dock magnification is not a spring. It is a distance falloff — scale 1 + 0.8·cos²(π·d ⁄ 2W) for a cursor d from an icon, out to W ≈ 2.5 icon widths, recomputed every mouse move with no easing at all. VESPER's main menu uses the same curve, because sitting in the Dock's position while magnifying differently is exactly the kind of near-miss that reads as cheap.

New animations only on your say-so — that was your instruction and it is recorded. The four springs above plus the Dock falloff are the entire motion vocabulary until you add to it.

06 · Ambience

Eleven modes, eleven images.

You sent eleven images and said to extract the feel and curate a theme per mode. Each image became exactly one mode. Grounds below are k-means clusters taken from the actual pixels in OKLab; accents come from the light source in each frame — the top 4% of pixels by brightness × chroma — because the mean of a night photograph is just the night. Both are then snapped to the nearest of your ten.

Click any mode to apply its ground and accent to this whole page — that is what a mode change does in VESPER, cross-fading on --smooth.

The images agree on something I did not expect. Mean chroma across all eleven is 0.04 — almost neutral. What makes them feel like anything is one small saturated thing against a large desaturated field: a single ember on a black horizon, one orange reflector on a black car, one gold band under a cold sky. So the ambience ground is always muted, and the accent is always the rare bright point. A mode that colours the whole screen has misread its own source.
