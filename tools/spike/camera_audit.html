4 · The two tests

A-008.4 — physical off stops capture at source

With the stream live, cover the lens completely with your thumb, then use whatever physical off the device has — a shutter, a slider, a case flap.

  1. Covering the lens: frames keep counting, luma drops toward 0. That is the sensor still capturing — it is not an off.
  2. A real physical off: frames stop incrementing entirely and the state turns red.

A-008.5 — is a capture indicator driven?

While the stream was live, did the device itself show that it was capturing — an OS dot in the status bar, a hardware LED beside the lens, anything not drawn by this page?

A-008.5 says a sensor whose capture indicator cannot be driven refuses to enter ACTIVE. "Nothing at all" is a real and useful answer — it means this device cannot be a perception rung as the corpus is written.
