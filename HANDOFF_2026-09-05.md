## RESOLVED, same session: the loop speaks

The owner chose SPEAK over notify on 2026-09-05, and it is built.
`agenda_trigger(handler=...)` now carries a handler supplied by the runtime,
which renders `agenda.sentence()` FIRST, offers the model the chance to phrase
it better, speaks it, and appends it to the transcript so the surface shows
what was said aloud. The model phrases and never decides -- what is due was
settled by the predicate and the ranking before the handler runs, so
INV-AUTO-2 holds; a model failure is a degradation to the deterministic line
and never a silence.

Measured against real `AutonomyLoop.dispatch`:

    WITHOUT handler (what shipped):  fired=1  dispatched=0  spoke=0
    WITH handler (now):              fired=1  dispatched=1  spoke=1
                                     said: "Dentist is next."

A HANDLER RATHER THAN AN `intent`, and the reason matters for whoever extends
this: an intent is free text handed to `Actor.maybe_act`, which proposes
CAPABILITY actions -- shell, files, sub-agents. Speaking is not one, so an
intent would reach a proposer with no way to satisfy it and come back "none".

Still open on this path: the announcement does not consult the interruption
budget, so it will speak whenever an item comes due regardless of the hour.
`LEAD_S` is 15 minutes, so in practice it speaks 15 minutes before a calendar
event -- fine while the owner is up, wrong at 3 a.m. `InterruptionBudget` is
already constructed in the runtime and this should route through it.

## The original correction, kept because the pattern outlives the fix

`trg_agenda_item_due` was registered with NO `handler` and NO `intent`.
`AutonomyLoop.dispatch` reads:

    if not intent:
        continue

so the trigger fires when something comes due, is dispatched, and falls
through. IT NOTICES AND DOES NOTHING. Item 5 above says the agenda "landed";
what landed is the half that can SEE. The half that ACTS was never written.

This is the same defect this session spent its whole length finding elsewhere
-- a wake listener nothing started, a task ranker nothing called, a correct
proposal that died at `KNOWN` -- committed by the session that was naming it.
Recorded here rather than quietly fixed, because the pattern is worth more
than the fix: **a subsystem is not landed until something reaches it**, and
"registered" reads like "reachable" in every listing that does not check.

The remaining design decision, which is why this was not rushed: `intent` is
free text handed to `actor.maybe_act`, so it goes through a model; a `handler`
is a plain callable that does not. Telling the owner what is due is a speech
act and probably wants the model; a bare notification does not. Whichever is
chosen, `core/agenda.py:sentence()` already renders the line.
